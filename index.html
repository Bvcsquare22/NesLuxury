Website proposal

A booking website for NES Luxury Apartments

Guests choose an apartment, pick their dates, and pay online in one place. You see every booking from a simple dashboard. Read through, make your choices, answer a few questions, and send everything back on WhatsApp.

01

What you get

  • Apartment pages with photos, descriptions, amenities and nightly prices for each of your Jahi apartments.
  • Live availability calendar so guests only see dates that are free. Once a booking is paid, those dates are blocked automatically, so there are no double bookings.
  • Online booking and payment through Paystack. Guests can pay by card, bank transfer, USSD or OPay.
  • Email confirmations sent to the guest and to you for every booking.
  • Your admin dashboard to see all bookings, guest details and payments, and to block dates yourself.
  • Works on every phone, since most of your guests will find you from Instagram on mobile.
  • WhatsApp button so guests with questions can still reach you directly.

Not included

Anything below can be quoted separately if you want it later.

  • A mobile app for the App Store or Play Store
  • Listing apartments owned by other people
  • Social media management or paid adverts
  • New features added after the design is approved
